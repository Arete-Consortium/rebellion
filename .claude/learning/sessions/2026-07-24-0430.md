## Mistakes and Inefficiencies
- **Stale lookup table**: When buffing base frigate HP in spawn.rs, forgot to update the identical hardcoded table in cg_campaign.rs. Caught by reviewer, not by tests.
- **Over-reliance on compiler**: Tests validate "enemies spawn with scaled HP" but bounds were loose enough (health > 40.0) to pass even with the bug. Tighter assertions (e.g., exact expected HP per type) would have caught it.

## Lesson Candidates
### Candidate 1
- Lesson: When two functions contain identical lookup tables, changing one without the other is guaranteed drift. Either consolidate into one table or add a CI check that compares them.
- Evidence: spawn.rs line 39-51 vs cg_campaign.rs line 283-289
- Scope: cross-project
- Confidence: high
- Destination: rule
- Promote now: yes

### Candidate 2
- Lesson: Test assertions for numeric tuning must be tight enough to catch the bug they're meant to prevent. "health > 40.0" was too loose to detect a 30% HP deficit.
- Evidence: cg_mission1_encounter.rs line 64
- Scope: cross-project
- Confidence: high
