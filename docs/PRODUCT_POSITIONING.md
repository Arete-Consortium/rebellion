# Rebellion — Product Positioning

**Version**: 1.0
**Date**: 2026-07-25
**Status**: Active — complements v2.0 vertical slice

---

## The Short Version

Rebellion is an arcade action game set in the EVE Online universe.
It asks: *What if the defining moments of New Eden were recreated as a polished, replayable, controller-friendly experience?*

Not an MMO. Not fan fiction. A complementary product for a gap CCP has little incentive to fill inside EVE Online itself.

---

## The Problem We Solve

EVE Online excels at:
- Persistent universe
- Player-driven economy
- Massive fleet warfare
- Sandbox gameplay
- Long-term progression

It deliberately does not excel at:
- 10-minute play sessions
- Fast arcade combat
- Couch/controller-friendly gameplay
- Replayable action campaigns
- Pick-up-and-play experiences

Those are not flaws. They are tradeoffs made in service of being an MMO. Rebellion optimizes for exactly the things EVE Online cannot optimize for without undermining its core identity.

### The Historical Simulation Framing

Rebellion is set inside the **CONCORD Capsuleer Combat Archive** — a military-simulator program that reconstructs defining conflicts of New Eden using authenticated battlefield telemetry, fleet records, and historical archives.

This framing is not lore flavor. It is **systemic architecture dressed as fiction**:

| Game System | In-Universe Explanation |
|-------------|------------------------|
| Mission replayability | "Run the simulation again. Improve your score." |
| Playing both sides | "Experience each faction's perspective." |
| Difficulty levels | "Adjust simulation fidelity." |
| Endless modes | "Stress-test the parameters." |
| Score attacks | "Evaluate combat efficiency against the archive." |
| Death without permanent loss | "It's a simulation. Learn from it." |
| Campaign DLC as new archives | "Unlock the EDENCOM Archive." |

The title makes sense: **Rebellion** is the archive's name for every conflict that shaped New Eden — the Minmatar Rebellion, the Caldari–Gallente War, the Triglavian Invasion, and more.

---

## Relationship to CCP's Visions of Greatness

CCP's *Cradle of War* expansion introduced Visions of Greatness — hyper-real simulations of historical battles inside EVE Online. This is validation, not competition.

| | CCP's Implementation | Rebellion |
|---|---|---|
| **Format** | Narrative Epic Arc inside EVE Online | Standalone arcade action game |
| **Session length** | Hours (MMO commitment) | 10–35 minutes |
| **Input** | Mouse + keyboard (PC MMO) | Controller-first, couch-friendly |
| **Death penalty** | Asset loss, time sink | None — simulation retry |
| **Replayability** | Linear narrative completion | Score chasing, leaderboards, endless modes |
| **Progression** | EVE skill training, ISK | In-session unlocks, ship upgrades, archive completion |

CCP is saying: *Players enjoy reliving New Eden's history.*
Rebellion is saying: *Let's make that its own product.*

## Target Audiences

### 1. Current EVE Players
- "I have 20 minutes before work."
- "I don't want to organize a fleet."
- "I just want to fly a Rifter and blow things up."

### 2. Former EVE Players
- They love the universe.
- They don't want the commitment anymore.
- They'd happily return for a focused action game.

### 3. People Intimidated by EVE
- They know the stories.
- They know the ships.
- The MMO feels overwhelming.
- Rebellion becomes an accessible entry point.

### 4. Arcade Shooter Fans
- They discover New Eden through gameplay first.
- Some may later become EVE Online players.

---

## Core Differentiator: Authenticity

The biggest risk is becoming a generic bullet-heaven with EVE skins. The defense is **authenticity**:

Every ship must behave like players expect:
- **Rifter** feels like a Rifter — fast, improvisational, projectile-based.
- **Caracal** feels like a Caracal — missile spam, shield tank.
- **Vexor** fights through drones.
- **Amarr** lasers feel distinct from **Caldari** missiles.

Faction identity is not cosmetic. It is mechanical:

| Faction | Combat Identity |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Caldari** | Missiles, shields, range control |
| **Gallente** | Drones, hybrid blasters, armor |
| **Minmatar** | Mobility, projectiles, improvisation |
| **Amarr** | Lasers, armor, sustained pressure |

Longtime EVE players must immediately recognize that the designers understand the universe. If a Merlin doesn't feel like a Merlin, the game loses its reason to exist.

## Monetization & Distribution (Tentative)

- **Base game**: Free vertical slice (Caldari–Gallente missions 1–3) as demo
- **Full campaigns**: Elder Fleet, Triglavian Invasion, Abyssal Depths as paid expansions / archive unlocks
- **Platform**: itch.io (web + native), Steam (if traction justifies the overhead)
- **License**: MIT (open source, community contributions welcome)

- [ ] Community evidence exists (Reddit, forums, Discord) that players have asked for an arcade EVE experience
- [ ] itch.io page copy reflects this positioning (not "space shooter," not "bullet heaven")
- [ ] In-game text and menu copy reinforce the Archive framing
- [ ] Marketing materials use "experience history" not "change history"

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

1. **"EVE but simpler"** — Rebellion is not a simplified EVE. It is a different product in the same universe.
2. **Generic bullet-heaven** — The mechanics must reinforce faction identity and lore.
3. **Simulation as headline** — CCP already owns "simulation of history." Market the *experience*, use simulation as explanation.
4. **Competing with EVE** — Frame as complementary. EVE players are the allies, not the enemy.
5. **Lore-dumping** — The Archive framing should be immediately understood in under 60 seconds. No pre-requisite reading.
